# DirectoR showcase

Every film here was made by DirectoR, on autopilot, in a sandbox with 4 CPU cores and no GPU.
No human touched the scripts, the shots or the edits.

| Film | Source given to DirectoR | Template | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `films/quantskill_launch_50s.mp4` (+ `_captions.mp4`, `.srt`, poster, script) | `QuantSkill.zip` (dropped on the Library page) | Launch film | Shiny app detected in the zip and launched; 5 screens scouted; README features matched to screens; the hook's words appear as they are spoken. |
| `films/quantskill_teaser_24s.mp4` | the same project, "More cuts › Teaser" | Teaser | Music-only, cut on a 120 BPM grid. Reuses the launch film's footage; "Predict. Prove. Report." comes from the README. |
| `films/orbit_launch_38s.mp4` (+ captions) | `inst/examples/orbit` (a folder path) | Launch film | bslib dashboard: slider drags, "Run forecast" click, table. |
| `films/orbit_social_17s_vertical_captions.mp4` | `http://127.0.0.1:…` (a URL), browser size *Mobile* | Social vertical | 9:16. Mobile footage in phone frames; the hamburger menu is opened on camera; bold word-by-word captions. |

`studio/` has screenshots of the app: Library, Source, Story (with the live preview), Shots, Sound, Look and Export.

About the voice: no neural voice could be downloaded in this sandbox, so the films use the offline
**Pico** voice, like QuantSkill's own films. On a Mac, DirectoR picks a macOS voice automatically
(Premium or Enhanced voices sound far better). Edge neural voices (free), OpenAI, ElevenLabs, Piper,
or your own recordings are one click away on the Sound page.
